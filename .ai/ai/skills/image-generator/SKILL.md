# Product Skill: Promotional Image Generator

## Purpose

Generate marketing-oriented images while preserving the important identity of the creator's model.

## Inputs

- reference image
- image type
- style
- optional scene
- plan/provider

## Image types

- hero
- lifestyle
- close-up
- desk setup
- promotional

## Rules

Do not invent product features or accessories and present them as included with the model.

Generated environments are marketing compositions, not factual product claims.
